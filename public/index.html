Community & Country

Working with Indigenous communities

We want to work alongside Aboriginal communities, Traditional Owners, Land Councils and Ranger groups to support the mapping, monitoring and documentation of Country - making geospatial technology more accessible while creating opportunities for Indigenous participation and technical development.

In Development

Indigenous Data Sovereignty

We are developing a product to support the culturally authorised mapping and protection of Country, including sacred and culturally significant sites. Our vision is to give participating communities greater control over the imagery and spatial information collected about their Country, through secure, permission-based access and community-governed data arrangements. The product will be developed in collaboration with participating communities and relevant rights holders, with cultural authority and community control guiding its design.
