Respect for Country

Cultural Authority & Indigenous Data Sovereignty

First Geospatial does not treat Indigenous ownership as automatic cultural authority over another group's Country. We design field workflows around the explicit permissions of local Traditional Owners, Land Councils, and authorized custodians.

Cultural knowledge and sacred geography remain strictly classified under client-approved access permissions. The Company asserts no ownership over cultural heritage simply because we collected or processed the raw spatial points.

01

Authorised land access permits and cultural briefing prior to mobilization.

02

Tiered data classification preventing unauthorized release of culturally sensitive sites.

03

Direct training and paid field participation for local rangers on their own Country.
