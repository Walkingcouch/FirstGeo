Capabilities & Deliverables

Enterprise Remote Sensing & Spatial Products

Enterprise Airborne LiDAR

Penetrating dense Northern vegetation canopies to generate calibrated point clouds, Digital Terrain Models (DTM), Digital Surface Models (DSM), and vertical canopy structural metrics.

High-Resolution Photogrammetry

Sub-centimetre RGB aerial acquisition, radiometric mapping, 3D textured mesh reconstructions, orthomosaics, and visual baseline monitoring for infrastructure and environmental assets.

Country & Research Mapping

Tailored field operations supporting university research trials, ranger groups, and Land Councils. Integrating repeat multi-temporal surveys for environmental and baseline studies.

Engineering & Volumetrics

Corridor mapping, cut/fill earthwork calculations, stockpile volumetrics, infrastructure route assessments, and site asset inspection in remote conditions.

GNSS & Survey Control

Independent dual-frequency GNSS and RTK ground checkpoint workflows ensuring geodetic repeatability, positional precision, and defensible QA/QC spatial deliverables.

Sovereign & Secure Data Handling

Strict onshore Australian processing and encrypted storage architecture aligned with DISP and Essential Eight security standards for sensitive cultural, government, and Defence clients.
