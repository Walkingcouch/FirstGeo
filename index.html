Indigenous Data Governance

Cultural Authority & Data Sovereignty

First Geospatial recognises that Indigenous ownership of our company does not grant automatic cultural authority over another group's Country. We design field capture and data handling around the formal authority of Traditional Owners and Land Councils.

All cultural data remains under the ownership of the traditional custodians. First Geospatial asserts no claim over cultural heritage or restricted knowledge simply because our sensors recorded the data points.
